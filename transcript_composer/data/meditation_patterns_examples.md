# Meditation Script Pattern Examples

> **Purpose:** Real transcript excerpts from the content library, organised by pattern. Each example is tagged with its Generation, Wound, Stage, Pillar, and Mood from the Excel repository so you can see exactly which context produced which phrasing. Use these as tone and language reference when writing new content.

> **How to use:** Find the pattern you are working with → read the examples → note the phrasing, pace, and emotional register → write new content in that register for your target Generation and Wound.

## `softening_settling_awareness`
**Family:** settling_grounding | **Placement:** middle

**What makes this pattern work:** The language never commands — it invites. "Allow", "notice", "soften" rather than "do", "feel", "breathe". The pace is slow. Each sentence is short enough to land before the next one arrives.

---

**Example 1** | Gen X · Invisible Sacrifice (GX-01) · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P01 Mindful Awareness · *carrying invisibly*
> allowing your body to soften to soften into the support beneath you now allow your breath to normalize and notice you have settled in and are ready to relax bringing your awareness to the top of your head notice any tension than the muscles around the scalp

*What to notice: "soften into the support" — not soften alone, but soften INTO something holding you. This is key for caregiver wounds where the body has been unsupported.*

---

**Example 2** | Boomer · Disconnection from Body (BO-04) · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P03 Somatic Healing · *stiff, tense, numb*
> have you ever had the feeling that you are lost in your head or like your head is in the clouds or as if you are disconnected from yourself and your surroundings I know I have when this happens I can use some grounding to bring my feet back down to earth and reconnect with myself

*What to notice: Opens with a question that names the wound without diagnosing it. Creates immediate recognition. Works for any "living above the neck" wound.*

---

**Example 3** | Boomer · Disconnection from Body (BO-04) · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P03 Somatic Healing · *stiff, tense, numb*
> close your eyes if you'd like or soften your gaze rest your hands in an easy effortless position together we will take a moment to pause calm down and reconnect to your energy this pause this time to check in can invite a sense of gratitude to come over you

*What to notice: "effortless position" — removes any performance from posture. The word "together" creates presence of the guide alongside the listener. Key for isolation wounds.*

---

**Example 4** | Boomer · Disconnection from Body (BO-04) · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P03 Somatic Healing · *stiff, tense, numb*
> allows it to relax and let go in a moment I will guide you through a relaxing scan of your body moving your attention strategically throughout your body to ground and restore your energy your body is safe and supported and knows what to do to relax

*What to notice: "your body knows what to do" — returns agency and intelligence to a body that has been ignored or overridden. Powerful reframe for Boomer and Gen X users.*

## `body_sensation_contact`
**Family:** settling_grounding | **Placement:** middle

**What makes this pattern work:** Very concrete, very physical. No metaphor yet — just sensation. The move from "where you meet the surface" to "edges blurring" is the transition from ordinary awareness to meditation state. Take it slowly.

---

**Example 1** | Gen Z · Belonging Without Roots (GZ-02) · Stage 1 Awakening + Boomer · Disconnection from Body (BO-04) · Stage 2 · P01 Mindful Awareness / P03 Somatic Healing · *ungrounded / stiff, tense, numb*
> notice where your body meets the surface below you what are the sensations of your outer body as it meets the outer environment notice what you are feeling at these points of contact what would it feel like for your edges to blur with the edges of your surroundings can you sense there is no difference between where you end and where the surface below you begins

*What to notice: This exact sequence — contact → boundary → blurring — is the transition from ordinary wakefulness into meditation. Use it when the user needs to land.*

---

**Example 2** | Millennial · Arrival Fallacy (ML-01) · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P02 Emotional Regulation · *disappointed, disoriented*
> notice any body pain with acceptance notice any body pleasure with acceptance settle into your body accept being in your body right now just as you are sit with yourself move closer to yourself accepting all that you are there is no need to make a change during meditation be just as you are

*What to notice: "move closer to yourself" — a beautiful instruction for identity and suppression wounds. The body becomes a place to move toward rather than away from.*

---

**Example 3** | Millennial · Authentic Self Suppression (ML-03) · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P06 Relational Wellbeing · *resentful, over-giving*
> as you are attending to the sensations and energy in your body take a moment to soften and release anywhere you can welcome a shift towards a safe

*What to notice: "welcome a shift towards safe" — not demanding safety, just welcoming the possibility of it. Essential phrasing for anyone in protection mode.*

## `grounding_settling_sitbones`
**Family:** settling_grounding | **Placement:** middle

**What makes this pattern work:** Sitbones are the most honest point of contact with the earth. Very few people have ever been guided to notice them. The specificity creates immediate presence. The sigh exhale is non-negotiable — it physiologically discharges tension.

---

**Example 1** | Boomer · Identity After Role (BO-03) · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P06 Relational Wellbeing · *finding new relevance*
> Feeling your sitbones and noticing how you're held. And on your next exhale, let yourself sigh and listen to your own sigh. Really take it in. You are enough, dear one. You have always been. The time is here to remember again.

*What to notice: "listen to your own sigh" — the sigh becomes an act of self-listening. The affirmation that follows ("You are enough") lands on a body that has just physically released. Sequence matters.*

---

**Example 2** | Boomer · Identity After Role (BO-03) · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P02 Emotional Regulation · *loss of structure*
> can let go of any emotions that you are holding on to let them go let go of tension let go of tightness or pain let go of the need to control or hold your posture often we are in fight-or-flight mode or under physical or emotional stress

*What to notice: Naming "the need to control your posture" is very specific to people who hold their bodies as performance. Boomer and Gen X users particularly.*

---

**Example 3** | Gen X · Cynicism as Shield (GX-04) · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P06 Relational Wellbeing · *contemptuous, dismissive*
> if you can't find or notice anything here other than the anger and frustration just sit patiently here and see if anything comes up

*What to notice: For cynicism wounds, do not try to dissolve the emotion — sit with it. This permission is the pattern. Do not rush past anger to get to peace.*

---

**Example 4** | Gen X · Invisible Sacrifice (GX-01) · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P01 Mindful Awareness · *carrying invisibly*
> notice where they may be holding on and on your next exhale allow your hips to soften allow them to let go giving in to gravity and sinking down further into the surface below you now letting your awareness drift down to your legs beginning with your thighs

*What to notice: Hips specifically — the pelvic floor and hips carry enormous emotional load, especially for caregivers and over-givers. Naming hips is not arbitrary.*

## `grounding_settling_posture`
**Family:** settling_grounding | **Placement:** pre_scan_settling

**What makes this pattern work:** Safety and comfort are stated explicitly before anything else. The user is given choice (seated or lying). No effort is required to maintain the position. This is the opposite of performing wellness.

---

**Example 1** | Boomer · Disconnection from Body (BO-04) · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P03 Somatic Healing · *stiff, tense, numb*
> I invite you to make yourself as comfortable as possible you can choose to be seated or lying down comfort and the feeling of safety are both very important for this practice choose a position that you don't have to use a lot of effort to stay in

*What to notice: "You don't have to use a lot of effort" — this is the key phrase. Removes the performative element of even sitting correctly.*

---

**Example 2** | Gen X · Emotional Self-Reliance (GX-02) · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P02 Emotional Regulation · *shut down, armored*
> I invite you to make yourself comfortable if possible be seated in a chair or on a cushion with your spine straight allow your hands to rest easily in your lap this can also be practiced standing with your bare feet on the earth for an even more grounding effect when you feel ready close your eyes allowing this to be a signal to your body and mind to invite relaxation and healing

*What to notice: "when you feel ready" — no countdown, no instruction. The closing of eyes is given meaning (a signal to the body). This works for armored users who resist being directed.*

---

**Example 3** | Millennial · Chronic Overextension (ML-02) · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P04 Deep Rest & Sleep · *cannot unwind*
> I invite you to make yourself comfortable it is important to know that you are safe and you are extremely comfortable in the position you choose you can choose to lie down on the floor or in your bed for this practice

*What to notice: Lying down is named explicitly. For overextended users, permission to lie down rather than "meditate properly" is itself a healing message.*

## `guided_meditation_intro`
**Family:** opening_intro | **Placement:** opening

**What makes this pattern work:** Names the experience that's coming without overselling it. The user knows what they are getting into. The invitation to close eyes or watch is important — it respects the listener's autonomy.

---

**Example 1** | Gen Z · Belonging Without Roots (GZ-02) · Stage 1 Awakening · P01 Mindful Awareness · *ungrounded*
> you are about to embark on a guided meditation journey over the next few minutes I will guide you into a deep state of relaxation where you will experience a wonderful calmness and meditative state of mind this time is for you and you alone you don't need to do anything except

*What to notice: "this time is for you and you alone" — resonates strongly for Belonging wounds where time for self is rare or guilt-inducing.*

---

**Example 2** | Gen Z · Belonging Without Roots (GZ-02) · Stage 1 Awakening · P01 Mindful Awareness · *ungrounded*
> you may wish to close your eyes or watch the screen or switch between both it doesn't matter which just do whatever feels right for you you are completely in control

*What to notice: "you are completely in control" — critical for Gen Z users with anxiety or Anticipatory Dread. Removes the vulnerability of surrendering control.*

---

**Example 3** | Millennial · Chronic Overextension (ML-02) · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P04 Deep Rest & Sleep · *cannot unwind*
> this practice can be used during the day to replenish your energy if you are feeling tired or to help you fall asleep and enjoy extremely productive and healing sleep using this systematic approach to full body and mind relaxation can help you connect with your own true and natural wisdom your ability for healing

*What to notice: Framing rest as productive ("healing sleep", "natural wisdom") is strategic for Millennial users who can only justify rest if it serves a purpose.*

## `somatic_scan`
**Family:** somatic_scan | **Placement:** middle

**What makes this pattern work:** Very slow. One body part at a time. The invitation to "feel from inside" rather than visualise is key — it activates interoception, not imagination.

---

**Example 1** | Boomer · Disconnection from Body (BO-04) · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P03 Somatic Healing · *stiff, tense, numb*
> with a relaxed awareness take a moment to slowly scan your entire body allow your awareness to move over each part of your body noticing any areas that if possible can be softened and released of tension

*What to notice: "if possible" — never demanding release. This is trauma-informed language. The body is never wrong for holding.*

---

**Example 2** | Boomer · Disconnection from Body (BO-04) · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P03 Somatic Healing · *stiff, tense, numb*
> become aware of the top of your head now can you feel this area from inside of your body as sensations only now move your attention from the top of your head to your forehead and feel a sense of softening and smoothing notice your right eye and relax the muscles around it

*What to notice: "from inside your body as sensations only" — not visualising the body from outside. This internal felt sense approach is the difference between relaxation and actual somatic work.*

---

**Example 3** | Boomer · Disconnection from Body (BO-04) · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P03 Somatic Healing · *stiff, tense, numb*
> allow this wave of comfort to continue its journey downward notice your right shoulder release and rest heavy and now notice your left shoulder and both shoulders together allowing your right arm elbow wrist and hand to sink down into the surface below you letting go completely through your left arm

*What to notice: The bilateral structure (right then left then both) is deliberate. It prevents the mind from going ahead and forces attention to stay with each part.*

---

**Example 4** | Boomer · Disconnection from Body (BO-04) · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P03 Somatic Healing · *stiff, tense, numb*
> since the area of your lower back linger on this area a little longer and ask your body for a sense of ease let go of all of the tension and tightness held in this area feel your body softening as each in-breath creates space for an easy flow of energy through your body

*What to notice: "ask your body for ease" — body is given agency and intelligence. This matters for users who have been overriding or ignoring their body.*

## `somatic_scan_exploration`
**Family:** somatic_scan | **Placement:** middle

**What makes this pattern work:** Shifts from directed attention to curious exploration. The listener stops following instructions and starts noticing for themselves. This builds self-directed awareness.

---

**Example 1** | Boomer · Identity After Role (BO-03) · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P06 Relational Wellbeing · *finding new relevance*
> What else can you notice with your five senses right now? Just explore whatever happens and whatever you notice. And when you're ready, take your time coming back to the world around you, stretching slowly, however you feel.

*What to notice: "however you feel" — no prescribed way to return. The return is as individual as the person. Essential for identity wounds where there is no single right answer.*

---

**Example 2** | Gen Z · Anticipatory Dread (GZ-03) · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P04 Deep Rest & Sleep · *hypervigilant*
> visualize your own body resting and become aware of your own physical presence allow the weight of your body and your bones to become heavy feel your bones sinking into the support allow your awareness to follow the sound of my voice and travel through your body on a journey of sensation simply feel each part as it is mentioned as you remain still and relax deeper

*What to notice: "bones becoming heavy" — this specific instruction activates the proprioceptive sense in a way that "relax" never does. Very effective for hypervigilant nervous systems.*

---

**Example 3** | Gen X · Emotional Self-Reliance (GX-02) · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P02 Emotional Regulation · *shut down, armored*
> let these positive powerful healing statements sink into your subconscious as truth for a moment longer feel the profound health coursing through your body and now bring your awareness back

*What to notice: The return phrase ("bring your awareness back") closes the somatic loop. Always end an exploration with a clear return.*

## `somatic_scan_awareness`

**Example 2** | Boomer · Disconnection from Body (BO-04) · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P03 Somatic Healing · *stiff, tense, numb*

---

**Example 3** | Boomer · Disconnection from Body (BO-04) · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P03 Somatic Healing · *stiff, tense, numb*
> since the feelings from within your body know that whatever you're feeling is okay this is about simply observing the sensations as they are invite a sense of ease into your body you may find that the simple act of paying attention to a specific body part

## `mind_reframing_kindness`

**What makes this pattern work:** Does not try to remove difficult feelings. Invites them in and sits with them. The opposites technique (noticing both ease and tension simultaneously) creates perspective without bypassing the wound.

> allow yourself to go further still into relaxation sense a safe place inside you a place where you feel secure and loved sense the place where your inner joy that you were born with is within you remaining as relaxed and calm as you can invite your imagination to call to mind a person a friend or loved one

*What to notice: "inner joy that you were born with" — reclaims joy as original, not something to be achieved. Powerful for identity wounds where the self has been lost under performance.*

---

**Example 2** | Boomer · Disconnection from Body (BO-04) · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P03 Somatic Healing · *stiff, tense, numb*
> give yourself permission to relax completely your mind may naturally drift off into thought that is okay you do not need to completely shut off your mind and empty it of all thoughts

*What to notice: "you do not need to empty your mind" — removes the most common reason people think they can't meditate. Especially important for Gen X and Boomer users who have been told they're "doing it wrong".*

---

**Example 3** | Boomer · Disconnection from Body (BO-04) · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P03 Somatic Healing · *stiff, tense, numb*
> with a relaxed awareness follow the scan of your body as your attention moves through areas of your body send the quality of love to each area your body knows how to relax and sometimes a simple invitation to do so is all it takes

*What to notice: "send the quality of love" not "send love" — the quality is softer, less demanding. The body is trusted to know how to relax.*

---

**Example 4** | Boomer · Disconnection from Body (BO-04) · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P03 Somatic Healing · *stiff, tense, numb*
> sense of replenished energy feel it in your body like muscle memory you can use this grounding meditation to balance your energy anytime you need you can use this technique to increase your energy if it's low or release any excess energy when it feels right to you

*What to notice: Giving the technique back to the user — "anytime you need". This builds self-efficacy, not dependency on the guide.*

## `breath_attention`
**Family:** breath_attention | **Placement:** middle

**What makes this pattern work:** The breath is the most reliable anchor because it is always present. The widening from breath to sound to sensation creates a gentle expansion of awareness without losing the anchor.

---

**Example 1** | Boomer · Disconnection from Body (BO-04) · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P03 Somatic Healing · *stiff, tense, numb*
> begin to broaden your awareness outward sense the air around you as it makes gentle contact with any exposed skin take in a full breath and let it out as slowly as you can and do this again breathing in and letting go of your breath releasing any held tension with it come back home to yourself to the sensations in your body to ground your energy

*What to notice: "come back home to yourself" — home is inside the body. This is the reframe for any disconnection wound.*

---

**Example 2** | Boomer · Disconnection from Body (BO-04) · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P03 Somatic Healing · *stiff, tense, numb*
> when you do recognize that your mind is drifting acknowledge your thoughts without following those thoughts into a drawn-out story invite your attention to come back to the sound of my voice and the sensations in your body to bring you back into the present moment

*What to notice: "without following those thoughts into a drawn-out story" — names what the distracted mind does. This is very relatable and reduces shame about mind-wandering.*

---

**Example 3** | Boomer · Identity After Role (BO-03) · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P06 Relational Wellbeing · *finding new relevance*
> just breathe and be. Begin to allow your awareness to start to notice sounds. Perhaps the sound of your own breath. And again, start to notice physical sensations like the feeling of clothes on your skin and the weight of gravity.

*What to notice: "just breathe and be" — three words that are the entire instruction. Minimalism is power for someone who has been over-doing.*

---

**Example 4** | Gen X · Cynicism as Shield (GX-04) · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P01 Mindful Awareness · *tension, locked down*
> let your attention move to your breath. Notice the sensations of your breath as it enters your nose. With your eyes closed, sense your breath as a felt experience. How does it feel to your body as this breath enters and how does it feel as it exits. Notice this breath as it carries the energy of the universe through this body.

*What to notice: "How does it feel?" — asking rather than telling. For cynicism wounds, questions respect the user's intelligence and autonomy more than instructions.*

## `closing_transition`
**Family:** closing_transition | **Placement:** closing

**What makes this pattern work:** The return is gradual. The listener is never abruptly pulled back. Kindness is expressed before the session closes. The tone matches the wound — no chirpiness after grief work, no solemnity after grounding.

---

**Example 1** | Boomer · Identity After Role (BO-03) · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P06 Relational Wellbeing · *finding new relevance*
> really luxuriating in your transition from this practice back to the rest of your day. Have a great day today, dear one.

*What to notice: "luxuriating in transition" — the return itself is a practice. Not rushing back. "Dear one" is a term of genuine warmth — not patronising when delivered sincerely.*

---

**Example 2** | Gen X · Cynicism as Shield (GX-04) · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P06 Relational Wellbeing · *contemptuous, dismissive*
> may you be peaceful may you be free from suffering and just sit here with this for the next few moments and now open your eyes thanks for sitting and I'll see you next time

*What to notice: "thanks for sitting" — very simple, no inflation. For cynicism wounds, grand closing statements feel hollow. Keep it honest and brief.*

---

**Example 3** | Gen X · Cynicism as Shield (GX-04) · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P01 Mindful Awareness · *tension, locked down*
> as you open your eyes and see everything a little bit differently as if there has been a shift an awakening to the self that you are thank you for practicing with me today I hope you have a wonderful day

*What to notice: "as if there has been a shift" — not claiming the shift happened, just opening the possibility. Respects the listener's autonomy to notice for themselves.*

## `mind_reframing_oneness`
**Family:** mind_reframing_oneness | **Placement:** middle

**What makes this pattern work:** The physical (gravity, weight) becomes the vehicle for the philosophical (oneness, freedom). Never abstract — always grounded in sensation first.

---

**Example 1** | Gen Z · Belonging Without Roots (GZ-02) · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P06 Relational Wellbeing · *wanting to be known*
> begin to notice this connection between you and the energy around you this connection allows your energy to expand and merge with the energy around you invite this connection and expansion

*What to notice: "invite" not "feel". No pressure. The connection is offered, not demanded.*

---

**Example 2** | Boomer · Legacy Uncertainty (BO-01) · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P06 Relational Wellbeing · *wanting to give while present*
> your mind is all one with nature you are part of the scene you are imagining in your mind like the leaves being released from the trees in fall you too can let go to make room for new possibilities to develop

*What to notice: The fall/leaves metaphor maps grief and release onto a natural cycle. Particularly powerful for Legacy and Grief wounds in Boomer users.*

---

**Example 3** | Gen Z · Belonging Without Roots (GZ-02) · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P06 Relational Wellbeing · *lonely, disconnected*
> let go into the weight of gravity and the blurring boundaries feel the freedom and expansion created by releasing the boundaries into vast spaciousness feel the openness of being one of all living beings connected with all others invite the sensations of freedom and spaciousness to expand outward and move all throughout your body sense your body is wide open and connected to the universal energy

*What to notice: This entire sequence — gravity → blur → expansion → oneness — is the pattern in pure form. Use it as a template.*

## `affirmation_release`
**Family:** affirmation_release | **Placement:** middle

**What makes this pattern work:** The affirmations emerge from the body's felt sense rather than being layered on top. They don't contradict the wound — they address the belief underneath it.

---

**Example 1** | Gen Z · Validation Dependency (GZ-04) · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P05 Cognitive Clarity · *exhausted by self-presentation*
> that you are safe and held. All is well. Let your human body know all is well no matter how it appears. Say to yourself "Thank you. I love you."

*What to notice: "no matter how it appears" — acknowledges that things may not look okay while affirming the underlying truth. Avoids toxic positivity.*

---

**Example 2** | Millennial · Authentic Self Suppression (ML-03) · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P06 Relational Wellbeing · *resentful, over-giving*
> i love you unconditionally i will always keep you safe you are important and special you are worthy of all that you desire i will always have a place for you in my heart

*What to notice: First person, addressed to self. The guide speaks as the listener's own inner voice. This is a powerful technique for suppression wounds where the inner voice has been silenced.*

---

**Example 3** | Millennial · Relational Erosion (ML-04) · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P06 Relational Wellbeing · *distant partner, distant self*
> choose a mantra or statement that resonates with you and promotes self-love acceptance and worthiness as you are solidifying your intention feel it in your heart as true

*What to notice: Giving the listener agency to choose their own affirmation — especially important for Millennial users who distrust prescribed mantras.*

---

**Example 4** | Boomer · Grief Unprocessed (BO-02) · Stage 1 Awakening · P01 Mindful Awareness · *grief held in body*
> without the falling of the leaves there can be no new growth it is your time to heal and let go and make room for new growth let go of anger let go of guilt let go of grief

*What to notice: The release is specific — anger, guilt, grief named separately. Specificity is more effective than "let go of what no longer serves" which is abstract.*

## `affirmation_gratitude`
**Family:** affirmation_release | **Placement:** middle

**What makes this pattern work:** Joy is not performed — it is located in a small real moment and then expanded. The progression is always small → larger, not immediate transcendence.

---

**Example 1** | Gen Z · Identity Diffusion (GZ-01) · Stage 2 Stabilisation + Gen Z · Anticipatory Dread (GZ-03) · Stage 2 · P04 Deep Rest & Sleep · *hypervigilant / numb*
> may your joy continue and grow may you live with joy and gratitude always may you appreciate the joy in your life may your joy continue and grow may you live with joy and gratitude always

*What to notice: Repetition is intentional — this is a blessing structure. The repetition creates rhythm and allows the words to sink in rather than be processed intellectually.*

---

**Example 2** | Gen X · Purpose Displacement (GX-03) · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P02 Emotional Regulation · *heavy, going-through-motions*
> now your whole body is relaxed and heavy on the earth find that you are fully relaxed you know you are safe and supported be open to guiding yourself into a calm state of mind now that you are fully relaxed you can begin this inquiry process

*What to notice: Gratitude here is quiet — the act of resting on the earth in safety is itself the grateful moment. Not every gratitude closing needs explicit words.*

---

**Example 3** | All generations · Cross-generational · Stage 2 Stabilisation · P03 Somatic Healing · *ambient, restorative*
> May all beings learn how to nourish themselves with joy each day. May all beings be able to live fresh, solid and free. May all beings not fall into the state of indifference or be caught in the extremes of attachment or aversion.

*What to notice: The Metta (loving-kindness) structure. Expands from self to all beings. Universal register — suitable for cross-generational content that does not target a specific wound.*
